# Test outputs

These are generated compatibility fixtures, not finished contest demo footage. All clips are 832×480 at 16 fps. Exact sampler settings and timings are recorded in [the Spark report](../SPARK_BRINGUP.md).

Recast motion comes from a modified excerpt of the **Sintel trailer**. Attribution: **© copyright Blender Foundation | durian.blender.org**. The excerpt was resized, cropped, retimed and transformed by Wan Animate. It is shared under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), with the [original source](https://download.blender.org/durian/trailer/sintel_trailer-480p.mp4) and [Blender's sharing terms](https://durian.blender.org/sharing/). The exported sample uses a matched Sintel still, recorded in [manifest.json](manifest.json). A separate manually aligned cartoon candidate failed lower-body visual QA and is excluded. Its [original reference](reference-character-original.png), [aligned reference](reference-character-aligned.png) and [manual transformation](reference-character-alignment.json) are retained for comparison. The app does not automatically align or retarget poses.

The Swap and Restyle clips use an original procedural red-ball scene and original illustrated reference images. Compare them with the [full ball source](source-ball-full.mp4); the [full Recast motion source](source-sintel-full.mp4) is also included.
